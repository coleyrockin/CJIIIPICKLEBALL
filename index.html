Frequently Asked Questions

How CJ's codes work, what they cost you (nothing), and where to find the latest.

How do I use a CJ's Pickleball discount code?

Copy the code from the deal card and paste it at checkout on the partner's site. For link-only deals, just tap Get Deal — the discount is applied automatically through CJ's referral link.

Do the codes cost anything?

No. Every code and link here is free to use. CJ may earn a small commission when you shop through them, at no extra cost to you.

Where do the deals come from?

They're real partnerships between CJ's Pickleball and pickleball brands. CJ curates and updates them from his Facebook page, so the codes here are the ones he's currently sharing.
